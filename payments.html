Payment Policy

Effective date: 10 August 2026

RIVAL PRAXIS UNIPESSOAL LDA provides automated business-to-business wholesale checkout for eligible orders billed in Portugal. Catalogue products, minimum quantities and prices are validated by our server, Portuguese VAT is calculated at checkout, and the exact amount is sent to REDUNIQ.

Accepted online payment methods

The payment methods available for an order are those displayed on REDUNIQ's secure hosted page. Availability may depend on the transaction, device, issuer and REDUNIQ approval. Payment credentials are never entered on or stored by the RIVAL PRAXIS website.

Cash is not accepted. Never send payment to account details received from an unverified source. Confirm any change of bank details with us using the contact information published on this website.

Instalment payments

Instalment plans may be accepted when necessary, subject to commercial assessment, order value and written approval. The deposit, payment dates, amounts, fees (if any), release of goods and consequences of late payment will be stated in the quotation or a separate written agreement. Instalments are not automatic and an order is not confirmed until the required initial payment has cleared.

Payment confirmation and refunds

An order is marked paid only after REDUNIQ confirms the final payment status, transaction identifier and exact server-calculated amount. A browser return alone is not payment confirmation. Approved refunds are returned, where reasonably possible, through the original payment method and may take additional processing time at the bank or provider.

Secure automated checkout

Eligible customers may use our automated checkout. After the order is validated, the customer is redirected to REDUNIQ to review the final amount and enter supported payment credentials. Payment status is verified by RIVAL PRAXIS before the order is recorded as paid.
