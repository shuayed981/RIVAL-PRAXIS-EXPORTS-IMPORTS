Payment Policy

Effective date: 1 August 2026

RIVAL PRAXIS UNIPESSOAL LDA supplies products on a business-to-business wholesale quotation basis. The accepted method, currency, schedule and payment instructions are confirmed in the applicable quotation or invoice.

Accepted non-cash payment methods

Subject to country, currency, transaction value, provider availability and approval, we may accept the methods expressly confirmed in the quotation. A method is available only after it has been activated for RIVAL PRAXIS by the relevant provider:

  • Domestic, SEPA and international bank transfers;
  • Credit, debit and commercial cards, including major card networks supported by our payment provider;
  • Secure online payment links and payment gateways;
  • Digital and mobile wallets supported by the selected provider;
  • Mobile or local electronic payment methods, including MB WAY or Multibanco where available;
  • SEPA direct debit, electronic bank payments or other traceable electronic methods agreed in writing;
  • Company cheque or another non-cash instrument only where expressly approved in advance.
Cash is not accepted. Never send payment to account details received from an unverified source. Confirm any change of bank details with us using the contact information published on this website.

Instalment payments

Instalment plans may be accepted when necessary, subject to commercial assessment, order value and written approval. The deposit, payment dates, amounts, fees (if any), release of goods and consequences of late payment will be stated in the quotation or a separate written agreement. Instalments are not automatic and an order is not confirmed until the required initial payment has cleared.

Payment confirmation and refunds

Orders may require full or partial advance payment. Payment is considered received only after cleared funds are confirmed. Customers are responsible for bank, intermediary, currency-conversion and payment-provider charges unless agreed otherwise. Approved refunds are returned, where reasonably possible, through the original payment method and may take additional processing time at the bank or provider.

Secure payment of approved quotations

Customers with an approved written quotation may use our secure quotation payment portal. The payable total is retrieved from the approved quotation record and cannot be changed in the browser. Card or supported payment credentials are entered only in the secure payment environment provided by the activated payment provider. A payment is treated as confirmed only after the provider result is verified.
