Shipping & Delivery Policy

Effective date: 1 August 2026

Wholesale orders only. Shipping method, cost, delivery estimate and applicable trade terms are confirmed in writing before the order is accepted.

Processing and delivery

Processing begins after order confirmation and receipt of any required cleared payment. Lead times depend on quantity, production or stock availability, destination and product specifications. The estimated dispatch and delivery windows shown in the quotation are estimates rather than guarantees unless expressly agreed in writing.

Carriers and tracking

Orders may be shipped by courier, road, air, sea freight or a customer-appointed carrier. Tracking or shipping documents are provided where available. Risk, title, insurance and responsibility for freight are determined by the agreed quotation and applicable Incoterm, if any.

Customs, duties and delays

The customer must provide accurate delivery and customs information. Import duties, VAT, customs charges, brokerage fees and local taxes are payable by the party identified in the quotation. RIVAL PRAXIS is not responsible for delays caused by customs, carriers, incorrect information, force majeure or events outside our reasonable control, but we will assist with reasonable enquiries.

Delivery inspection

Customers should inspect packaging and goods promptly, record visible damage with the carrier where possible, and notify us of damage, shortage or incorrect goods within five business days of delivery.
