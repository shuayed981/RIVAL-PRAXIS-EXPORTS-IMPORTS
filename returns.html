Returns & Refund Policy

Effective date: 1 August 2026

This policy applies to business-to-business wholesale orders. Customers must inspect goods on delivery and report damage, shortages, incorrect goods or suspected defects within five business days, including the order reference, affected quantity, description and clear photographic evidence.

Return authorization

No goods may be returned without prior written authorization and return instructions from RIVAL PRAXIS. Approved items must be unused, unaltered, securely packaged and returned in the condition specified in the authorization. Unauthorized returns may be refused.

Non-returnable goods

Custom-made, personalized, altered, clearance or specially sourced goods are generally non-returnable unless defective, incorrectly supplied or otherwise agreed in writing. A change of mind does not automatically create a return right for wholesale business orders.

Inspection and remedy

After inspection, an accepted claim may result in repair, replacement, credit note, partial refund or refund, as commercially appropriate and subject to the order agreement. Unless the goods are confirmed defective or incorrectly supplied, return transport and related charges are normally the customer's responsibility.

Refund timing

Approved refunds are initiated within 14 business days after inspection and approval. Additional bank or provider processing time may apply. Refunds are normally issued through the original payment method, less any agreed or non-refundable charges.
