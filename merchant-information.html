How orders and payments work

  1. The customer selects catalogue products and completes the company and billing details at checkout.
  2. RIVAL PRAXIS validates the product references, sizes, minimum quantities, prices and Portuguese VAT on the server.
  3. The exact order amount is sent securely to REDUNIQ's hosted payment page.
  4. Payment credentials are entered only in REDUNIQ's secure environment.
  5. The order is marked paid only after REDUNIQ confirms the final status, transaction reference and exact amount.

Available online methods are those displayed by REDUNIQ for the transaction. We do not accept cash and we never ask customers to send complete card details by email or telephone.
