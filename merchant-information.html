How orders and payments work

  1. The customer requests a wholesale quotation.
  2. RIVAL PRAXIS confirms availability, quantity, product specifications, taxes, shipping, currency and the final payable total in writing.
  3. The customer accepts the quotation and receives approved payment instructions.
  4. Where online payment is available, payment credentials are entered only in the activated payment provider's secure environment.
  5. An order is confirmed only after payment has been verified.

Available methods depend on the approved quotation, destination and activated provider. We do not accept cash and we never ask customers to send complete card details by email or telephone.
